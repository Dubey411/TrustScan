📐 Indian Deterministic Mathematical Rules (Zero Hallucination)

1. Aadhaar Dihedral Group D5 Verhoeff Formula:
c = ∑ p(i mod 8, d_i) = 0 (Validated using non-commutative D5 multiplication)
2. PAN 10-Character Structural Syntax:
[A-Z]{3} + [P/C/H/F/A/T] + [A-Z] + [0-9]{4} + [A-Z]
(Character 4 strictly validates individual vs corporate entity)
3. MCA 21-Digit Corporate Identity Number (CIN):
[L/U] + [5-digit Industry] + [2-char State] + [4-digit Year] + [PTC/PLC] + [6-digit RoC]
4. Image Error Level Analysis (ELA) Tamper Formula:
Δ_ELA = | I_original - I_recompressed@90% | × 10

🔒 Privacy, Zero PII Retention & Compliance

TrustScan enforces strict cryptographic data sanitization:

  • In-Memory Processing: Scanned documents are never persisted to disk or permanent databases.
  • Aadhaar Masking: First 8 digits are masked in UI and logs (XXXX XXXX 1234) in full compliance with UIDAI regulations.
  • Stateless Compute: Document buffers are garbage-collected immediately after score generation.
